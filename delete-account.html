Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how to delete your Kliniko account and the personal data associated with it, what is removed, what we may need to keep, and how long it takes.

Effective date: October 14, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Overview

You can request deletion of your Kliniko account and its associated personal data at any time, free of charge. There are two ways to do it: directly in the App (fastest), or by submitting a request through the project's public tracker if you no longer have access to the App.

Deleting your account is permanent. Once the deletion is complete you will not be able to recover the account, its content or your progress, and the same email address or username may be reused by someone else.

2. Option A — delete your account in the App

If you can still sign in, this is the fastest route:

  1. Open the Kliniko app and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open your Profile (or the Settings / Account area, depending on your app version).
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Read the confirmation screen and confirm. You may be asked to re-enter your password or complete a sign-in check to prove the request is genuine.
  5. You will see a confirmation that the deletion request has been accepted.

Menu labels can differ slightly between app versions and platforms. If you cannot find the option, use Option B below.

3. Option B — request deletion on the web

If you cannot access the App or cannot find the in-app option, submit a deletion request using the project's public issue tracker:

  1. Go to github.com/Senj1u/Kliniko/issues/new (a free GitHub account is required to open an issue).
  2. Set the title to Account deletion request.
  3. Include the information listed in the template below, then submit the issue.
Title: Account deletion request

Please delete my Kliniko account and the personal data associated with it.

- Account email (or username) used in the App:
- Platform (Android / iOS / other):
- Approximate date the account was created:

representative, and that I understand the deletion is permanent.

Please do not post passwords, identity documents or other sensitive information in a public issue. We will reply on the issue to confirm the request and, if necessary, to arrange a private channel for identity verification.

4. What is deleted, and what may be retained

When your request is verified, we delete or irreversibly de-identify the personal data linked to your account. The table below summarises the outcome for each category of data.

Outcome of a completed account deletion
Data Outcome
Account record (name, username, email address, password hash) Deleted.
Profile information and profile photo Deleted.
Content you created in the App Deleted from active systems.
Preferences and app settings Deleted.
Usage and analytics data De-identified or aggregated, or deleted. It is no longer linked to you.
Crash and diagnostic logs Deleted or left to expire on their normal 90-day cycle.
Support correspondence Deleted unless still needed to resolve an open request.
Encrypted backups Overwritten within the normal backup rotation cycle (normally up to 90 days). Backups are not used for any other purpose.
Records we are legally required to keep Retained for the period required by applicable law, then deleted. Retained records are isolated and not used for other purposes.
Minimal records needed to prevent fraud and abuse Retained only where necessary and only for as long as necessary (for example, to prevent a deleted account from being reused abusively).

5. How long deletion takes

  • In-app deletion: the account is deactivated immediately and the data is deleted or de-identified within 30 days.
  • Web request: we acknowledge the request as soon as we can, normally within a few business days. If identity verification is needed, the 30-day deletion clock starts once verification is complete.
  • Backups: residual copies are removed as the backup cycle rotates, normally within 90 days.

6. Delete some data without deleting your account

You do not have to delete your whole account to reduce the data we hold. Depending on the version of the App you are using, you can usually:

  • delete individual items of content you created, using the delete option on that item;
  • update or clear optional profile fields, and remove your profile photo;
  • turn off optional permissions (such as notifications or camera access) in your device settings;
  • ask us to correct or delete specific pieces of information by contacting us — see section 7.

7. Questions and contact

If you have any question about deleting your account or your data, or if you want to check the status of a request, contact us:

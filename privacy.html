1. Who we are and scope

Kliniko is a mobile application. This policy applies to the App and to this legal pages website (together, the "Services"). It does not apply to any third-party websites, apps or services you may reach through the Services, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

Kliniko is an independent project maintained by the developer identified in the project's GitHub account. Where this policy refers to "we", "us" or "our", it means that developer, who acts as the data controller (or equivalent term under your local law) for the information described below.

2. Summary

  • We collect the information you provide when you create an account and use the App, plus limited technical information generated automatically by your device.
  • We use that information to operate, maintain, secure and improve the App.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • We share information only with service providers that help us run the App, or where the law requires it.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, and you can delete your account at any time — see Account & Data Deletion.

This summary is provided for convenience only. The full sections below are the binding description of our practices.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide directly

  • Account details — such as your name, username, email address and authentication credentials. Passwords are stored only in hashed form, or are handled entirely by the sign-in provider you choose.
  • Profile details — any optional information you choose to add to your profile, such as a photo, date of birth or contact details.
  • Content you enter — information, notes, records, appointments, messages or other content you create, upload or submit while using the App.
  • Support communications — the content of messages you send us when you request help, report a problem or otherwise contact us.

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and technical data — device model, operating system and version, app version, language, time zone, and coarse region (for example, derived from an IP address at city or country level).
  • Usage data — which screens or features you use, timestamps, and in-app events, collected in aggregate or pseudonymised form.
  • Diagnostics — crash reports and performance data that help us find and fix defects.

3.3 Information from third parties

If you sign in using a third-party account (for example, Google or Apple sign-in), we receive basic profile information from that provider — such as your name, email address and an account identifier — in accordance with the permissions you grant.

3.4 Information we do not ask for

We do not ask you to submit government identification numbers, financial account numbers or precise real-time location. Please do not include such information in free-text fields, and do not submit information about other people unless you have their permission to do so.

4. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • create and manage your account and provide the core features of the App;
  • personalise your experience and remember your preferences;
  • operate, maintain, troubleshoot and secure the Services, and detect, prevent and address fraud, abuse or security incidents;
  • understand how the App is used so that we can improve it and develop new features;
  • communicate with you about service messages, updates, and responses to your requests;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not use your information to make decisions that produce legal effects about you, and we do not use it for automated profiling for advertising purposes.

5. Legal bases for processing

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the App and the features you request, and to manage your account.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the Services secure, prevent abuse, and improve and develop the App, balanced against your rights and expectations.
  • Consent — where we ask for it, for example for optional communications or device permissions. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose information to comply with the law.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising purposes. We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers. Vendors that help us run the Services, such as cloud hosting and storage, authentication, crash reporting, analytics, email delivery and customer support tooling. They may access information only to perform services for us and are bound by contractual confidentiality and data protection obligations.
  • Legal and safety. Where we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law, legal process or a governmental request, or is necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of users, the public or us.
  • Business changes. If the project is reorganised, transferred or merged, information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any change in ownership or of a material change to this policy.
  • With your direction or consent. For example, if you use a feature that shares content with other users or to a third-party service you select.

Aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably be used to identify you may be shared for research, analytics or product improvement.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

This legal pages website is a set of static pages and does not set cookies of its own and does not run advertising or third-party tracking scripts. The App may use local storage or similar on-device technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences and cache content. Where the App uses an analytics or crash-reporting service, that service may collect the technical and usage data described in section 3.2.

You can limit or reset identifiers through your device settings (for example, by resetting your advertising identifier or restricting tracking in your operating system's privacy settings).

8. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, and then delete or de-identify it. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the reason we hold it:

Indicative retention periods
Category Typical retention
Account and profile information While your account is active, and deleted or de-identified within 30 days of a verified account deletion request (subject to section 11).
Content you created in the App While your account is active; deleted with your account, unless you delete it earlier.
Usage and analytics data Up to 26 months, in aggregate or pseudonymised form.
Crash and diagnostic logs Up to 90 days.
Support correspondence Up to 24 months after the request is resolved.
Records we must keep by law For the period required by applicable law (for example, tax or accounting records).

Residual copies may remain in encrypted backups for a limited period before they are overwritten in the normal course of our backup cycle.

9. How we protect information

We use technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. These include encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), access controls that limit who can reach production data, hashed storage of passwords, and logging and monitoring for suspicious activity.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, so please choose a strong, unique password and keep your credentials private. If we become aware of a breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights in relation to your personal information:

  • Access — to confirm whether we process your information and obtain a copy of it.
  • Correction — to have inaccurate or incomplete information corrected. Much of this you can do yourself in the App.
  • Deletion — to have your information erased. See Account & Data Deletion.
  • Portability — to receive the information you provided in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Objection and restriction — to object to, or ask us to restrict, certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, to withdraw it at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing already carried out.
  • Non-discrimination — not to be treated differently for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise a right, contact us using the details in section 16. We may ask you to verify your identity before acting on a request. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law (typically 30 days). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and the personal data associated with it at any time. Step-by-step instructions, including what is deleted and what may be retained, are published separately at Account & Data Deletion.

When we receive a verified deletion request, we delete or de-identify the associated personal information within 30 days, except where we are required or permitted by law to retain specific records, or where limited information must be kept to prevent fraud and abuse or to resolve open disputes. Any such retained data is kept only for the period required and is not used for any other purpose.

12. Children's privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age of digital consent in your country, which may be up to 16). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details in section 16 and we will delete it promptly.

If the App is made available to minors in a supervised setting, the responsible adult or institution is accountable for obtaining any consent required by local law.

13. International data transfers

We may store and process information in countries other than the one in which you live, including countries whose data protection laws differ from your own. Where we transfer personal data out of the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, an adequacy decision, or your consent, and we take steps to ensure your information receives an adequate level of protection.

You can ask us for more information about the safeguards we use for international transfers by contacting us using the details in section 16.

14. Third-party services and links

The Services may link to, or integrate with, third-party services — for example app stores, sign-in providers or hosting providers. Those services are operated by independent companies and are governed by their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, and we encourage you to review the policies of any third-party service before using it.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, our practices or applicable law. When we make a change, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where the change is material, provide a more prominent notice (for example, an in-app notice or an email). We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the Services after an update takes effect means you accept the revised policy.

Earlier versions of this policy can be requested using the contact details below.

16. How to contact us

If you have questions about this policy, or wish to exercise any of your privacy rights, please contact us:

  • Privacy requests & questions: github.com/Senj1u/Kliniko/issues
  • Maintainer: github.com/Senj1u
  • Account deletion: see the deletion instructions

Please do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. If your request requires confidential details, say so and we will arrange a private channel.
